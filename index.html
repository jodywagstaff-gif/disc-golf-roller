    How to play

    1. 01

      Let it spin.

      Tap BEGIN DISC, then BEGIN STABILITY, then BEGIN SHOT. Each reel starts only when you’re ready and stops on its own.

    2. 02

      Catch it early.

      QUICK STOP works only during the first third (about 1.27 seconds). Then let the reel stop itself. Wild card? You choose.

    3. 03

      Go throw.

      Add players in Setup. Finish a challenge, then pass to the named next player. Save everyone’s strokes in Scores before moving to the next hole.

    Lowest score on the previous hole tees off first. Ties keep the earlier order. Missing scores hold the next hole. Pausing a reel never resets its Quick Stop deadline.

    Reduced motion uses a still display with the same automatic stop and manual control. Wild cards allow all options. Progress saves on this device; visit online once before using offline.
